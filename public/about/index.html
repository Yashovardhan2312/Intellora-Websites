About us

Intelligence, reimagined

Intellora Innovations exists to put practical artificial intelligence to work for businesses of every size.

Our mission

We believe the best use of AI is the quiet kind: forecasts that prevent waste, workflows that save hours, and decisions made with better information.

We build AI-led solutions and workflows that solve a specific, measurable business problem, and we start with the industries where better decisions have an immediate impact.

Our first product

ForkSight helps the hospitality industry reduce food and inventory wastage by predicting demand from seasons, trends and sales. It is in active development, and we welcome conversations with early users.
