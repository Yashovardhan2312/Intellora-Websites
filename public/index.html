ForkSight: waste less, earn more

Over-ordering means spoiled stock. Under-ordering means empty shelves and lost sales. ForkSight is built to predict what your kitchen will need, using seasons, trends and your own sales history.

  • Designed to reduce food and inventory wastage

How it works

From sales data to smarter orders in three steps

  1. Connect your data

    Bring in your sales and inventory information. No need to change how you run your outlet.

  2. AI forecasts demand

    Models learn from seasonality, trends and your sales patterns to predict what you will need.

  3. Act on recommendations

    Get clear stock suggestions so you can order with confidence and keep waste low.

Why it matters

Built to help hospitality businesses protect their margins

Less waste

Order closer to real demand so less food is thrown away and less money is lost.

The right stock

Fewer stockouts on popular items, with less capital tied up in slow ones.

Healthier margins

Lower purchasing waste and better planning add up to more profit from the same sales.
