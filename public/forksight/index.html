ForkSight

AI-powered inventory forecasting for hotels, restaurants and cafés. Built to cut food and inventory waste, and help you earn more from every order.

Request early accessHow it works
The problem

Ordering by gut feel is expensive

Hospitality demand shifts with the season, the weather, local events and trends. Plan too much and food spoils. Plan too little and popular dishes run out. Either way, margin disappears.

Most kitchens still plan stock from memory and spreadsheets. ForkSight is designed to replace guesswork with forecasts.

The solution

Forecast demand, then order the right amount

ForkSight learns from your sales history together with seasonality and trends, then recommends how much stock to hold. The goal is simple: waste less, stock out less, and protect your profit.

Capabilities

What ForkSight is built to do

Demand forecasting

Predicts what you will need using your sales history, trends and AI/ML models.

Seasonality aware

Accounts for seasons and recurring patterns so peaks and quiet periods are planned for.

Stock recommendations

Clear guidance on what to order, hold or reduce, so every purchase has a reason.

Waste reduction

Helps you spot over-ordering early so less food is lost to spoilage.

Profit focus

Designed around the number that matters most: what you keep after costs.

Uses data you have

Starts from the sales records you already keep, with no heavy new process to learn.

How it works

Three steps from data to decisions

  1. Connect

    Share your sales and inventory data.

  2. Forecast

    AI predicts demand using seasons, trends and your sales.

  3. Act

    Follow stock recommendations and watch waste fall.
