The Problem

HORECA runs on guesswork. That's expensive.

Most food and beverage businesses order inventory based on experience, habit, and gut feel. The result? Over-ordering leads to spoilage and wasted capital. Under-ordering leads to stockouts, unhappy guests, and lost revenue. Both problems stem from the same root cause: no reliable way to predict demand.

The Solution

ForkSight turns your sales history into a procurement advantage.

ForkSight analyses your historical sales data, seasonal patterns, menu composition, and operational context to generate item-level demand forecasts. These forecasts power clear, prioritised stock recommendations — so your team always knows what to order, how much, and when.

Capabilities

Everything you need to run a leaner, smarter kitchen.

Item-level demand forecasting

Forecasts broken down by individual menu item or ingredient — not just aggregate volume. Know exactly which items will spike and which will slow.

Seasonality & trend detection

ForkSight automatically identifies weekly, monthly, and seasonal patterns in your data so forecasts stay accurate as your business evolves.

Procurement recommendations

Receive prioritised order lists with suggested quantities — ready to hand to your supplier or purchasing team with confidence.

Waste reduction insights

Identify the items most prone to over-ordering and spoilage. ForkSight surfaces patterns that help you tighten your purchasing over time.

Planning horizon flexibility

Generate forecasts across daily, weekly, or monthly horizons depending on your operational planning cycle and supplier lead times.

Simple data onboarding
